Creating Impact
Where It Matters Most

The Raajratna Foundation was born with a vision to enhance the welfare of society through comprehensive charitable initiatives. Our foundation focuses on diverse areas including health, education, environmental sustainability and social empowerment aiming to create a positive and lasting impact on communities in need.

The Foundation was established in 2019 by Mr. Naresh Sanghvi and Mr. Ashwin Shah. We believe that by working together, we can create a more equitable, healthy, and sustainable future for all. Raajratna Foundation collaborates with various health organizations to offer medical help to underprivileged patients.
