What we do

Founded in 2019, Raajratna Foundation leads the Group's Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives with a steadfast commitment to creating meaningful and sustainable social impact. Our work focuses on healthcare, education, environmental sustainability, and social upliftment, addressing some of society's most pressing needs through thoughtful and community-driven initiatives.

From mobile medical units and clean energy donations to scholarship programmes, environmental conservation, and community welfare projects, every initiative is designed to improve lives, empower communities, and create lasting positive change. Guided by compassion, responsibility, and a vision for inclusive development, our goal remains clear—to uplift lives where it matters most and contribute towards building a healthier, more equitable, and sustainable future for all.

Global goals for
sustainable development

Raajratna Foundation, our initiatives are aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), reflecting our commitment to creating a healthier, more inclusive, and sustainable future. Through focused efforts in healthcare, education, environmental sustainability, renewable energy, women empowerment, elderly care, sports development, and social welfare, we contribute to global goals by addressing local community needs and driving meaningful, long-term impact.
