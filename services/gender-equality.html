What we do

Gender Equality

RRV empowers women through healthcare, education, vocational training, livelihood opportunities, and initiatives that promote financial independence and equal participation.

Driving Impact
Across Key Areas

RRV works towards gender equality by addressing barriers that limit women's participation in education, employment, entrepreneurship, and community life. The Group supports initiatives that provide women with access to vocational training, livelihood opportunities, healthcare, and financial independence.

By promoting skill development, confidence, leadership, and equal opportunities, RRV aims to empower women to participate fully and equally in society.

  • Empowering women through skills and livelihood opportunities
  • Promoting equal access to education, healthcare, and employment
  • Building confidence, leadership, and financial independence
